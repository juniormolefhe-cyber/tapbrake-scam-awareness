# Incident Report Template

This template is for anyone who has received a scam message, answered a suspicious call, tapped a scam link, or shared account details. Use it to write down what happened before reporting it.

- Did you share a PIN, password, or one-time code? Yes / No
- Did you send money? Yes / No
- How much money was involved, if any?

## Evidence

- Screenshot the message or call record.
- Do not delete the SMS, WhatsApp message, email, or call log.
- Write down the phone number, email address, or link used by the scammer.
- Save any payment confirmation or bank alert.

## Who to contact

- Bank or mobile money provider: Use the number on your card or in the official app.
- Botswana Police Service: 999

## Notes

Write anything else that may help, such as the exact wording of the message, the name used by the caller, or the account number you were told to pay into.
